## Proposed bounded scope

Keep existing identity and the preference/competition services authoritative initially. Payload can edit campaign templates, audience descriptions, show/season editorial descriptions and scheduling proposals. Domain commands must own subscriber state, consent history, send authorization, applications, team membership, results and bracket advancement. There is no need to migrate these domains merely to replace the catalogue resolver layer.

For an isolated email experiment, add an authenticated internal Workers HTTP endpoint accepting a template revision, audience/channel, recipient reference and idempotency key. Persist an outbox/job ID in D1 and enqueue a reference; the worker re-checks current suppression/consent immediately before a transport call. The local transport records synthetic messages only. No real recipient addresses, provider credentials, mail bindings or automatic sends belong in that experiment. Separate campaign approval from enqueue, accepted from delivered, and timeout/unknown outcome from failure; retries must not promise exactly-once delivery without provider idempotency/reconciliation.

For competition exploration, start with one synthetic show, season, solo bracket, team bracket, four entrants and a complete result progression. Retain the public GraphQL facade shapes while delegating commands to a competition service. Represent `MatchSide` through its existing `kind` field, not a newly introduced union. Preserve UTC instants, round/position ordering, stable IDs and show/season/bracket slugs.

## Acceptance tests and gates

1. **Identity and access:** account and email-only subscribers never merge implicitly; another user's preferences, registrations, invites and participation are inaccessible. Verify the subject/subscriber mapping before implementing `me` preference fields.
2. **Consent state:** import active rows and history together; absence of an active row must never be interpreted as consent. Preserve suppression/unsubscribe during replay and restore. Repeated commands are idempotent; stale subscribe events cannot overwrite newer unsubscribe events.
3. **Unsubscribe behavior:** old links and new scoped signed references work; account/email and audience/channel boundaries remain explicit. Unsubscribe after enqueue but before delivery suppresses the queued marketing message. Failed preference storage never reports confirmed success. Synthetic tests cover malformed, duplicate, replayed and expired requests.
4. **Queue behavior:** duplicate enqueue, duplicate delivery, worker crash after transport acceptance, retry exhaustion and dead-letter replay produce auditable states without silently sending twice. Consent-store outage suppresses marketing delivery. Service/transactional categories follow an explicit policy rather than an arbitrary bypass.
5. **Competition integrity:** preserve show tenancy, uniqueness, team size/entry limits, deadline rules, reviewed application states, immutable audit references and referential integrity. Reject a winner outside the match and cross-season entrants. Simultaneous/replayed results must not create duplicate rounds or schedules.
6. **Public parity:** snapshot `Bracket`, `BracketSeason`, `BracketMatch`, `MatchSide`, participation types, query arguments and Show extensions; compare source ordering, DateTime output, missing/null behavior and authorization using synthetic data. Keep catalogue mutation routes separate.
7. **Migration:** stage a read-only projection first, compare state at a watermark, then transfer one bounded authority. No dual writes. Rehearse rollback with post-cutover changes and a real failure-injection test before connecting production consumers.

## Rollback and dependencies

Email rollback must stop new enqueueing, drain/quarantine unknown jobs, retain transport receipts/deduplication keys, and replay every suppression and preference event after the baseline into the restored authority. A database restore must not reactivate people who unsubscribed after the backup. Leave unsubscribe handling available while campaign sending is stopped.

Competition rollback must pause administrative result/team commands, retain an ordered command journal and ID mapping, reconcile results and advancement, and restore the last verified schedule while retaining new accepted registrations. Raw restoration of old bracket rows would lose legitimate outcomes and can regenerate matches; it is insufficient.

Dependencies are verified Academy identity, an explicit subscriber namespace bridge, trusted service ingress, event ordering/idempotency, synthetic private-data fixtures, queue/transport test doubles, a bounded retention policy, stable legacy URLs and independent domain authorization tests. Unknowns include exact live client usage, delivery provider capabilities, operational consent semantics, deployed writer versions and the intended meaning of “custard.” These are **stretch blockers only** and do not block the isolated catalogue POC.
