# Academy website copy

- Never use the term "field report" in website copy, metadata, assets, enquiry options, or generated public output. Use plain descriptions of the hands-on work.
- Offer one Partnership across Awareness, Adoption, and Support. These are areas of work, not separately priced products. Bespoke projects and community challenges have their own scopes. Keep the page, form, email, metadata and shareable brief consistent.
- Publish numerical audience and outcome claims only with a traceable source and an observation date. Separate organic distribution from paid amplification.
- Keep customer case studies factual and permissioned. Private email feedback is research evidence, not permission to publish a testimonial.
