# Academy website copy

- Never use the term "field report" in website copy, metadata, assets, enquiry options, or generated public output. Use "Hands-on content" for the paid Awareness offer.
- Keep the public service names "Awareness, Adoption, and Support" and "Adoption review" consistent across the page, form, email and shareable brief.
- Publish numerical audience and outcome claims only with a traceable source and an observation date. Separate organic distribution from paid amplification.
- Keep customer case studies factual and permissioned. Private email feedback is research evidence, not permission to publish a testimonial.
