## Design context

Current direction: the Panda/Ark Academy system documented in `DESIGN.md`.
Use the live semantic tokens and recipes in `packages/design-system`, not an
older paper/spruce palette or Instrument Serif/Inter Tight type system.

### People and purpose

Working cloud-native engineers and platform teams come here to understand
systems, find a lesson, evaluate a tool, and learn from practitioners. They need
clear choices, readable technical material, and honest information.

### Character

Candid, technical, human. Red Hat typography, cool surfaces, navy text and a
restrained pink accent. Real content and authored composition carry the brand.
Keep borders quiet, hierarchy decisive, and action labels specific.

### Decisions

- Every visible element needs a job.
- Prefer open layouts and ruled sections over nested boxes.
- Show actual content artwork and people; generic News imagery stays generic.
- Remove repeated metadata and actions rather than disguising them.
- Keep critical content available without JavaScript.
- A source check or one viewport screenshot is not a complete visual review.
- Use the explicit audit inventory and ledger; never infer all-page sign-off.
