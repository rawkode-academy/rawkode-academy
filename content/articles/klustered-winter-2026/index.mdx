Klustered is back. Winter 2026 starts on Friday, 30 October.

Contestants race against the clock to diagnose and fix deliberately broken Kubernetes clusters. The community can sabotage the clusters, so the rest of us get to watch the troubleshooting unfold live.

Want to have a go? Head to the [Klustered Apply page](/shows/klustered/apply) and click **Apply** to check the current entry options.

Put 30 October in your calendar. We’ll see who can get the cluster back on its feet.
