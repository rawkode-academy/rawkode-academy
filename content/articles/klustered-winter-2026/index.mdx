Klustered is back. Winter 2026 starts on Wednesday, 11 November at 17:30 GMT with a pre-season, then the Solo bracket in January.

Contestants race against the clock to diagnose and fix deliberately broken Kubernetes clusters. The community can sabotage the clusters, so the rest of us get to watch the troubleshooting unfold live.

Want to compete? Applications for Winter 2026 are open. Click [Apply](/shows/klustered/apply), choose Solo or Team, and sign in to submit your application.

Put Wednesday, 11 November, 17:30 GMT in your calendar. We’ll see who can get the cluster back on its feet.
