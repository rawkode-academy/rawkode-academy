Portainer says 2.45 LTS is the final release in the 2.x line and that [3.0.0 will be the initial 3.x STS release](https://www.portainer.io/blog/portainer-3-0-is-coming). Its [September product strategy](https://4731999.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/4731999/Portainer%20Product%20Strategy%20-%20September%202026.pdf) targets the next LTS release for December, but neither source identifies that release as 3.3.0.

Community Edition will stay on the 2.x codebase rather than receive a separate 3.x build. Portainer says it will provide security fixes and bug fixes for CE and BE 2.45 LTS, and backport 3.x capabilities that have an equivalent Docker API, for as long as that LTS is supported. Portainer says 3.x will continue to manage native Docker, Swarm and Podman environments, although capabilities that depend on Kubernetes primitives cannot have Docker feature parity.

Portainer's [current lifecycle table](https://docs.portainer.io/start/lifecycle) marks 2.45 LTS as supported and maintained through May 2027. However, it still lists planned 2.x releases through 2.51 LTS, which conflicts with the announcement that 2.45 is the final 2.x release. Portainer has not reconciled those two published positions.

Community access to 3.x is directed through the [three-nodes-free Business Edition offer](https://www.portainer.io/take-3), limited to one licence per organisation. Its [licence](https://www.portainer.io/legal/3nf-license-agreement) permits internal business use on no more than one server and three total nodes, and prohibits hosting, service-bureau use, or using it to provide services to third parties.

**Operator Action:** Treat the 2.x release schedule as unsettled, verify which Docker-dependent features are covered by backports, and check the 3NF terms against your use case before planning a 3.x upgrade.
